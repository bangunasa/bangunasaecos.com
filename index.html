About BAE

One team, from the first sketch to the final handover.

PT. Bangun Asa Eksplorasi is a Design and Build company established in 2025. Two core divisions, Design and Construction, work in close collaboration to deliver seamless, fully integrated projects.

BAE brings designers and builders to the same table. We draw it, then we build it, so what is decided on paper is carried through on site.

Our name carries what we stand for.

Bangunto build

Our commitment to actively shaping and building every project with purpose and care.

Asahope

Our dedication to turning each client's vision into reality through thoughtful design and precise execution.

Eksplorasiexploration

Our drive to keep learning, innovating, and improving, always looking for better ways to create meaningful, lasting spaces.

Andreas Christian
Licensed Architect · Member of IAI (Ikatan Arsitek Indonesia) · Co-founder

A graduate of architecture who went on to complete his professional licensure, Andreas spent years at a design consultancy before co-founding BAE. His practice is built on the conviction that architecture should not be imposed. It should belong to those who inhabit it.

"A building should never feel like a beautiful alien. It should know its people, fulfilling their dreams through every detail, and leaving a quiet, lasting impact on every living being around it."
Elang Herlambang
Civil Engineer · MSc. Sustainability Construction · Co-founder

Holding a degree in civil engineering and a master's from the University of Bristol in Sustainability Construction, Elang brings years of industry experience to BAE. He co-founded the company with a clear mission: to make the construction industry a place of true human dignity.

"From the ground worker to the senior professional, everyone in this field deserves to prosper. My work is to advance every tier of this industry until that becomes the standard, not the exception."
