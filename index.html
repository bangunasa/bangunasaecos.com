About BAE

We Create
Spaces That
Endure

PT. Bangun Asa Eksplorasi, established in 2025, is a Design and Build company that builds ideas into reality through two core divisions—Design and Construction—working in close collaboration to deliver seamless and fully integrated project outcomes.

True to our name, Bangun reflects our commitment to actively shaping and building every project with purpose and care. Asa represents our dedication to turning each client's vision into reality through thoughtful design and precise execution. Meanwhile, Eksplorasi embodies our drive to continuously learn, innovate, and improve—always seeking better ways to create meaningful, functional, and lasting spaces.

Andreas Christian
Licensed Architect

A graduate of architecture who went on to complete his professional licensure, Andreas spent three years at a design consultancy before co-founding BAE. His practice is built on the conviction that architecture should not be imposed — it should belong to those who inhabit it.

"A building should never feel like a beautiful alien. It should know its people — fulfilling their dreams through every detail, and leaving a quiet, lasting impact on every living being around it."

Elang Herlambang
Civil Engineer · MSc. Sustainability Construction

Holding a degree in civil engineering and a master's from the University of Bristol in Sustainability Construction, Elang brings four years of industry experience to BAE. He co-founded the company with a clear mission: to make the construction industry a place of true human dignity.

"From the ground worker to the senior professional — everyone in this field deserves to prosper. My work is to advance every tier of this industry until that becomes the standard, not the exception."
