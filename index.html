Andreas
Christian
Licensed Architect · Member of IAI (Ikatan Arsitek Indonesia) · Co-founder
Design

A graduate of architecture who went on to complete his professional licensure, Andreas spent years at a design consultancy before co-founding BAE. His practice is built on the conviction that architecture should not be imposed. It should belong to those who inhabit it.

"A building should never feel like a beautiful alien."

Elang
Herlambang
Civil Engineer · MSc. Sustainability Construction · Co-founder
Build

Holding a degree in civil engineering and a master's from the University of Bristol in Sustainability Construction, Elang brings years of industry experience to BAE. He co-founded the company with a clear mission: to make the construction industry a place of true human dignity.

"Everyone in this field deserves to prosper."
