About BAE

We Create
Spaces That
Endure

PT. Bangun Asa Eksplorasi, established in 2025, is a Design and Build company that builds ideas into reality through two core divisions—Design and Construction—working in close collaboration to deliver seamless and fully integrated project outcomes.

True to our name, Bangun reflects our commitment to actively shaping and building every project with purpose and care. Asa represents our dedication to turning each client's vision into reality through thoughtful design and precise execution. Meanwhile, Eksplorasi embodies our drive to continuously learn, innovate, and improve—always seeking better ways to create meaningful, functional, and lasting spaces.
